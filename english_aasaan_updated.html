Tenses in Hindi — आसान गाइड

English grammar ko examples ke saath samjhein.

GRAMMAR

Tenses English grammar ka zaroori hissa hain. Tense se pata chalta hai ki koi kaam kab hota hai—abhi, pehle, ya aane wale samay mein. Hindi se English seekhte waqt rules ratne ke bajay meaning, sentence pattern aur roz ke examples samajhna zyada madadgar hai.

English mein teen main times hain: Present (वर्तमान), Past (भूतकाल), aur Future (भविष्य). Har time ko aam taur par chaar forms mein padhaya jata hai: Simple, Continuous, Perfect aur Perfect Continuous. Is tarah 12 common tense patterns bante hain. Har conversation mein sabhi forms zaroori nahi hote, lekin unhe samajhne se sentence banana aasaan hota hai.

1. Present Tense — वर्तमान काल

Present tense aadat, routine, general facts, abhi chal rahe kaam aur haal hi mein poore hue kaam batane ke liye use hota hai.

Present Simple

Aadat, routine aur facts ke liye. Hindi mein aksar “ता हूँ”, “ती है”, “ते हैं” jaisa arth hota hai.

Pattern: Subject + base verb (he/she/it ke saath verb + s/es)
I go to school. — मैं स्कूल जाता/जाती हूँ।
She reads every day. — वह हर दिन पढ़ती है।
The sun rises in the east. — सूरज पूरब से निकलता है।

“I play” lekin “He plays”. Negative ke liye do not/does not aur question ke liye Do/Does use karein: “She does not play.”

Present Continuous

Bolte waqt jo kaam chal raha ho, uske liye.

Pattern: Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing
I am eating. — मैं खाना खा रहा/रही हूँ।
They are playing. — वे खेल रहे हैं।
He is studying now. — वह अभी पढ़ रहा है।

I ke saath am, he/she/it ke saath is, aur you/we/they ke saath are lagta hai. “I eating” galat hai; “I am eating” sahi hai.

Present Perfect

Kaam poora ho chuka ho aur uska sambandh abhi se ho, toh Present Perfect use ho sakta hai.

Pattern: Subject + has/have + verb ka third form (V3)
I have finished my work. — मैंने अपना काम पूरा कर लिया है।
She has arrived. — वह पहुँच गई है।

He/she/it ke saath has aur I/you/we/they ke saath have aata hai. Finished, eaten aur written V3 ke examples hain.

Present Perfect Continuous

Kaam pehle shuru hua aur ab tak jaari hai; yeh form duration par zor deta hai.

Pattern: Subject + has/have been + verb-ing + since/for
I have been studying for two hours. — मैं दो घंटे से पढ़ रहा/रही हूँ।
She has been working since morning. — वह सुबह से काम कर रही है।

Since starting point ke liye (since Monday), aur for duration ke liye (for two days) use hota hai.

2. Past Tense — भूतकाल

Past tense pehle hue kaam ya sthiti batata hai. Yesterday, last week aur two days ago jaise time words context ko clear karte hain.

Past Simple

Pattern: Subject + verb ka second form (V2)
I visited Delhi yesterday. — मैं कल दिल्ली गया/गई।
She cooked dinner. — उसने रात का खाना बनाया।
They played cricket. — उन्होंने क्रिकेट खेला।

Regular verbs mein aksar -ed lagta hai: play/played. Irregular verbs alag hote hain: go/went, eat/ate, see/saw. Negative aur question mein did ke baad base verb aata hai: “I did not go”, “Did you go?”

Past Continuous

Past ke kisi khaas waqt par jo kaam chal raha tha, uske liye.

Pattern: Subject + was/were + verb-ing
I was sleeping at 10 p.m. — मैं रात 10 बजे सो रहा/रही था/थी।
They were watching TV. — वे टीवी देख रहे थे.

I/he/she/it ke saath was aur you/we/they ke saath were aata hai. Background action ke liye: “I was cooking when he called.”

Past Perfect

Past ke ek kaam se pehle poora ho chuka doosra kaam batane ke liye.

Pattern: Subject + had + V3
The train had left before we arrived. — Hamare pahunchne se pehle train ja chuki thi.

Har purane kaam ke liye Past Perfect ki zaroorat nahi. Aksar do past events ka order clear karne ke liye use karein.

Past Perfect Continuous

Past ke kisi point se pehle koi kaam kuch samay se chal raha tha, toh yeh form duration batata hai.

Pattern: Subject + had been + verb-ing + since/for
I had been waiting for an hour when the bus came. — Bus aane tak main ek ghante se intezar kar raha/rahi tha/thi.

3. Future Tense — भविष्य काल

Future tense aane wale samay, plans aur predictions ke baare mein batata hai. “Will” ke alawa “going to” aur Present Continuous bhi future plans ke liye use hote hain.

Future Simple

Pattern: Subject + will + base verb
I will call you tomorrow. — मैं तुम्हें कल फोन करूँगा/करूँगी।
It will rain. — बारिश होगी।

Will ke baad base verb aata hai: “She will come”, “She will comes” nahi.

Future Continuous

Future ke kisi khaas waqt par jo kaam chal raha hoga.

Pattern: Subject + will be + verb-ing
At 8 p.m., I will be studying. — रात 8 बजे मैं पढ़ रहा/रही होऊँगा/होऊँगी।

Future Perfect

Future ke kisi samay tak kaam poora ho chuka hoga.

Pattern: Subject + will have + V3
I will have finished the report by Friday. — मैं शुक्रवार तक रिपोर्ट पूरी कर चुका/चुकी होऊँगा/होऊँगी।

Future Perfect Continuous

Future ke kisi point tak kaam kitne samay se jaari hoga, is par zor dene ke liye. Yeh form rozmarra ki baat-cheet mein comparatively kam aata hai.

Pattern: Subject + will have been + verb-ing + duration
By June, I will have been working here for five years. — जून तक मुझे यहाँ काम करते हुए पाँच साल हो चुके होंगे।

4. Tenses yaad karne ka aasaan tareeka

Sabhi rules ek saath yaad karne ke bajay har din ek form par focus karein. Present Simple se shuru karein, phir Present Continuous aur phir Past aur Future ke basic forms seekhein. Har rule ke teen apne examples likhein—positive, negative aur question.

Hindi sentenceEnglishForm
मैं रोज़ पढ़ता हूँ।I study every day.Present Simple
मैं अभी पढ़ रहा हूँ।I am studying now.Present Continuous
मैंने पढ़ाई की।I studied.Past Simple
मैं कल पढ़ूँगा।I will study tomorrow.Future Simple

Examples ko zor se bolkar practice karein. Sirf word-by-word translation na karein; poora English pattern samjhein. Kuch verbs, jaise know, believe aur own, aam taur par continuous form mein har context mein natural nahi lagte.

5. Aam galtiyan aur unse bachav

Galti 1: “He go to work.” Sahi: “He goes to work.” Present Simple mein he/she/it ke saath verb ka form yaad rakhein.

Galti 2: “I am go to school.” Sahi: “I go to school” (routine) ya “I am going to school” (abhi ki activity). Ek sentence mein bina wajah am aur base verb saath na lagayein.

Galti 3: “Did you went?” Sahi: “Did you go?” Did ke baad base verb aata hai.

Galti 4: Since aur for ko ulta use karna. “Since 2022” starting point hai; “for three years” duration hai.

6. Rozana practice plan

Roz 10–15 minute nikaalein. Pehle 3 minute rule padhein, agle 5 minute paanch sentences banayein, phir 3 minute unhe bolkar record karein aur aakhir mein chhota quiz karein. Hafte ke ant mein purane examples dohrayein. Galti hona learning ka normal hissa hai—unhe note karke sahi sentence phir se bolna progress mein madad karta hai.

Nateeja: Tenses English ko samajhne aur apni baat saaf kehne ka tool hain. Har sentence ko kisi formula mein zabardasti fit karne ke bajay context par dhyan dein. Regular reading, listening aur speaking ke saath dheere-dheere sahi tense chunna aasaan ho jayega.

Quick revision: Present = abhi/aadat; Past = pehle; Future = aage. Simple = fact ya event; Continuous = chal raha action; Perfect = kisi point se sambandh ya completion; Perfect Continuous = duration par focus.
